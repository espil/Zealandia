## 1. Listing title (hard limit, verified)
- Han's canonical title "Espil Online: Zealandia Extension Pack" is 38 characters.
- Both stores cap the app name/title at 30 characters: Google Play title 30, Apple App Store app name 30 (Apple subtitle is a separate 30-character indexed field; Google Play short description is 80 characters).
- So the full phrase cannot be the title on either store, but nothing is lost: the "Extension Pack" wording moves to the subtitle / short description, where it is still indexed for search on Apple.

Recommended (one title, both stores):
- Title: `Espil Online: Zealandia` - 23 characters. Keeps the Espil Online brand first (matches espil.online and han.espil.online naming) and Zealandia as the product word.
- Apple subtitle: `Zealandia Extension Pack` - 24 characters, under the 30 limit. Carries Han's exact "Extension Pack" phrase into Apple's indexed fields.
- Google Play short description: `The Zealandia Extension Pack for Espil Online: battle the sunken continent` - 74 characters, under the 80 limit. Carries the full phrase plus the genre hook.

Alternatives, if Han prefers a different emphasis (all verified under 30):
- `Espil Online: Zealandia Pack` - 28 characters. Closest to the canonical phrase that fits as a title alone.
- `Zealandia: Espil Online` - 23 characters. Leads with the game name instead of the brand; weaker brand continuity with espil.online.
- `Zealandia Extension Pack` - 24 characters. Drops "Espil Online" from the title entirely; not recommended, the brand is the search asset Han is building with the SEO burial strategy.

## 2. Price point (break-even math, 15% small-business commission as recorded in the build plan)
First-year store fees to recover: Apple Developer Program $99/year + Google Play $25 one-time = $124.

- $4.99 - net about $4.24 per sale - about 30 sales to cover the $124
- $6.99 - net about $5.94 per sale - about 21 sales to cover the $124
- $9.99 - net about $8.49 per sale - about 15 sales to cover the $124 (recommended)
- $12.99 - net about $11.04 per sale - about 12 sales to cover the $124 (same price point as the Vancouver City Sprite Pack on itch.io)

Recommendation: $9.99. It is a one-dungeon v1 with housing deferred to v1.1, so it should price below the $12.99 sprite pack (a finished, complete asset product) while still covering the yearly Apple fee in about 15 sales - consistent with Han's $0-recurring-cost rule, since the paid app is what covers the Apple fee per the build plan. If Han wants the launch to read as premium from day one, $12.99 is the defensible alternative; $4.99 underprices a paid extension whose web hub is already free.
